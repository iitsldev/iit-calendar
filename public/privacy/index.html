Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 29, 2026

Welcome, and thank you for your interest in Sati (“Sati”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how the legal entity International Institute of Theravada collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Sati mobile application (the “App”), our application website at https://sati.theravado.com/, and our organization website at https://theravado.com/.

Sati is designed as an offline-first, reverent companion application for Buddhist monastics and lay practitioners. It provides astronomical solar calculations for Vinaya observance, multi-tradition Uposatha tracking, multi-script Pāli chanting, and meditation timers. We are deeply committed to user privacy, data minimization, and transparency.

1. Personal Information We Do Not Collect

We believe that spiritual practice should remain completely private. We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personal information.

  • No Account Registration: You are not required to create an account, register an email address, or provide any personal details to use the App.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics platforms, user tracking tools, behavioral profiling, or advertising networks.
  • No Cloud Syncing of Personal Activity: Your meditation sessions, chant bookmarks, notes, and usage history remain strictly on your device.

2. Device Permissions and On-Device Processing

The App requests access to certain device features solely to provide essential offline features:

  • Location Data: To determine accurate sun times (such as dawn Aruṇuggamana and solar noon Majjhanhike) essential for monastic Vinaya meal rules, Sati may request access to your device’s location coordinates (latitude and longitude). All astronomical calculations are performed entirely locally on your device using client-side algorithms. Your location coordinates are never sent to external servers or shared with any third party.
  • Notifications: If enabled, notification permissions are used solely to trigger on-device reminders (such as dawn alerts or meditation timer bells). The App does not send or receive remote push notifications.

3. Local Storage and Data Retention

All application preferences, selected tradition settings, chanting audio downloads, and study texts are stored locally on your device using standard sandboxed storage mechanisms (Local Storage and IndexedDB). This data is stored only on your hardware and is never synced to external servers.

4. Data Deletion and Your Choices

Because no personal information is collected or transmitted to external servers, International Institute of Theravada does not hold or retain any of your personal data on remote servers.

You maintain complete control over all data stored on your device:

  • You can clear all stored preferences, bookmarks, and downloads at any time via your device settings (e.g., Settings > Apps > Sati > Storage > Clear Data).
  • Uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally saved data from your device.

5. Third-Party Services and Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, trade, or disclose your information to any third parties. There are no advertising SDKs, commercial trackers, or third-party marketing tools embedded within the App or Website.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App and Website are intended for general audiences of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect, request, or maintain any personal information from children or any other users.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date.

Unless otherwise indicated, International Institute of Theravada is the legal entity and developer responsible for the processing of information described in this Policy.
